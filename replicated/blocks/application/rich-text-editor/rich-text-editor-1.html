Loomwell 4.2 Launch Brief

Loomwell 4.2 brings scheduled replies and a rebuilt inbox search to every workspace on October 14. This brief is the source of truth for positioning, rollout and support readiness. Live numbers sit on the rollout dashboard.

Goals

  • Cut median first reply time for teams over 50 seats by 20%.

  • Move inbox search from the Scale plan to every plan, including Free.

  • Keep launch week tickets under 1,200, down from 1,640 at the 4.0 launch.

  • Publish both help center articles before general availability.

Rollout

  1. Dogfood with the support team from October 1.

  2. Open to 10% of workspaces on October 7 while search latency is watched.

  3. General availability on October 14, with the changelog and the customer email.

Half my team works overnight. Scheduling replies means nobody sends a 3 a.m. apology again.

Priya Raman, support lead at Fieldnote

Pricing does not change in 4.2. Leave the Q1 pricing review out of every piece of launch copy.

Launch Checklist

  • Final copy for the in-app announcement

  • Search latency dashboard shared with the on-call rotation

  • Help center article for scheduled replies, owned by Daniel

  • Support macros updated for the new search operators

Search Operators

Search now reads from:, status: and quoted phrases. Paste this into the macro as the example:

from:maya status:open "refund request"
