Inbox Search Rollout

At this point in time, the plan is to roll out the new inbox search to every workspace in a number of stages, in order to make sure that we are able to catch regressions well before they have a chance to reach our largest customers.We will release inbox search in four stages, so regressions surface before they reach our largest customers.

Search ships with Loomwell 4.2 on October 14. This plan covers each stage, the metrics that gate it and who signs off.

Stages

  1. Internal: the Kestrel Works support team, from September 29.

  2. Beta: 40 opt-in workspaces, from October 6. Beta admins will recievereceive a short survey after their first week.

  3. General availability: every workspace on October 14.

  4. Enterprise: workspaces over 500 seats, one week after GA.

Success metrics

It is important to note that a stage can only move forward when all of the numbers below have held steady for a period of three consecutive days.A stage moves forward only after these numbers hold for three straight days.

  • Median query time under 300 ms, p95 under 900 ms.

  • Zero result rate below 8 percent on the top 200 queries.

  • No rise in tickets tagged search against the prior week.

Any stage pauses on its own if a rollback has occured in the last 24 hours.

Risks

Index rebuilds for the largest workspaces take up to 6 hours, so enterprise accounts move last. The rebuild runbook still needs an owner (TBD).

Support needs a seperate macro for search feedback before beta starts. Who writes the macro copy, Daniel or Maya?
